# Exercise 2 - Implement a New Feature

> For this exercise pick someone from your team to act as a product owner for Electric Wealth. They want to add a RRSP calculator to the application. The BA should ask questions of the PO (the PO can just make up the answers). Here are some starting requirement ideas
1. The RRSP calculator must allow user to enter their annual income
2. The calculator must allow users to enter how much money they want to contribute to their RRSP
3. The calculator must show users how much they could save in taxes by contributing to their RRSP
4. The calculator must allow users to sel;ecty their province or territory to caclulate tax savings
5. The calculator must show users how much their RRSP savings could grow over time
